Each xlsx file contains data for calculating II-, DD- and ID-adder. In each sheet, every row is cell length in one cycle. The first 3 columns are current cycle ID and the two daughter-cycle IDs. Notably, each row in 'II-adder' contains two cycles, corresponding to the two newly formed origins. 
In the two DnaA upshift assays, medium shift was performed at 1632 min in Experiment 260301, and at 1683 min in Experiment 260622.
